# 🎬 Movie Stream Finder

Welcome to the **Movie Stream Finder Agent**!

### 📽️ Your Personal Cinema Assistant
I help you find where to watch your favorite movies across the globe. Whether you're at home or using a VPN to travel digitally, I'll find the best streaming options for you.

### 🎨 Cinematic Experience
This app is designed with a classic theater aesthetic:
- **Theater Red** for actions and energy.
- **Oscar Gold** for the highlights you deserve.
- **Rich Black** for that deep, immersive cinema feel.

### 🍿 How to use
Just type the name of a movie, and I'll find its details. Once found, click the **"🍿 Where to Stream?"** button to see availability across services like Netflix, Amazon Prime, HBO Max, and more!
